Data release from the Claude v1.1.2 review (item 3), built from `main` by Dev Desk.

- **Stress:** 14,473 words whose only accent is a single pashta now have stress source `postpositive-single`. One pashta means final stress by rule. The stressed letter is unchanged. The review queue (`default-ultima`, `build-report/stress-review.csv`) drops from 23,293 to 8,820 words.
- **One pashta convention:** the 8 corrected words that UXLC writes with two pashtas now use OSHB's qadma + pashta (for example Lev 13:3, 1 Sam 9:21, Jer 2:31). No word in `surface` carries two pashtas. Stress stays `doubled`, and the pause tree is unchanged.
- **Morphology of L word divisions:** every main word now has a lemma and a morph.
  - The two split words (Gen 14:17 `01h5Cb`, 2 Sam 3:30 `10wPXb`) carry their OSHB proper name.
  - The 8 merged words join both OSHB analyses, for example Num 12:9 `c/2734/639` `HC/Vqw3ms/Ta`.
  - New field `morph_note` (JSON and SQLite) says where each one came from.
- **Word-division policy:** the 10 L word divisions are the documented exception to the editorial class, with `decision` = `divide` (ASBUILT).

Counts: 23,213 verses, 306,779 words (1,278 qere), uxlc-diff 44 rows. Prose rhythm 99.38%, poetry 95.9%, Psalms 94.3%, all unchanged from v1.1.3.
